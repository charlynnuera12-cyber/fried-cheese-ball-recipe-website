HOW TO MAKE FRIED CHEESE BALL?

1. Scoop 2 teaspoons of dough and roll into a ball. If desired, roll balls in cracker crumbs for an extra crispy coating.

2. Now shape the dough into lime-sized balls. Use roughly 2 tablespoons of the mixture per cheese ball. To ensure they’re all the same size, you could use a measuring scoop.

3. Place all the cheese balls on a plate and refrigerate for at least 30 minutes. This is very important - it prevents the cheese from leaking out while frying and helps them hold their shape better.

4. If desired, roll the cheese balls in crushed cracker crumbs for an extra crispy coating. This adds a nice crunch to the outside!

5. In a medium frying pan, heat oil over high heat (350°F for deep frying). Fry cheese balls until golden brown, turning once, about 30-60 seconds per side.

6. Remove the cooked cheese balls with a slotted spoon and place them on a paper towel-lined plate to drain excess oil.

7. Serve the cheese balls hot while they're still crispy on the outside and melted inside. You can serve them with marinara sauce, ranch dressing, or sweet chili sauce for dipping.
